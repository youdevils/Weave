What is FacetFold?

A model of how your work fits together.

Complex work is made of many connected things. A process uses a system. A team is responsible for it. A capability depends on both.

  • Processes
  • Systems
  • People & teams
  • Capabilities
  • Relationships
  • Anything particular to your domain

FacetFold lets you describe those things and how they relate as a structured, living model. You decide the types and the attributes that matter, and every part of the model is checked against them. It is something you maintain over time, not a diagram you draw once.

  • ProcessOnboard a new customer
  • uses
  • SystemCustomer records
  • owned by
  • TeamCustomer operations
Why FacetFold

A model you can rely on.

Understand the whole

See how the different parts of complex work connect, from a single process to the systems, teams and capabilities around it.

Keep the model trustworthy

Changes are proposed, checked and reviewed before they are committed, with evidence and history so you can see why things are the way they are.

Explore the connections

Investigate relationships and dependencies, and trace where each piece of the model came from.

Share what you’ve built

Publish a self-contained, interactive model that opens in any browser and can be shared independently of FacetFold.

How it works

Build, govern, understand, publish.

  1. Build

    Create a model from a ready-made starting point, or import the data you already have.

  2. Govern

    Make changes through proposals, with validation, evidence and history behind every one.

  3. Understand

    Explore relationships, dependencies and provenance across the whole model.

  4. Publish

    Create a portable, interactive model that you can send to anyone.
