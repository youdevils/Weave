# Help & documentation — initial content inventory

An authoring roadmap, not published documentation. Titles and one-line purpose
statements only; articles are written one at a time, starting with Getting
started. See `website/help_articles/README.md` for how to add an article once
one of these is ready to write.

## 1. Getting started

1. **What is OnyxJar?** — Orient a new user: what a living model is and who OnyxJar is for.
2. **Creating your first workspace and model** — Walk through the first-run setup end to end.
3. **Roles and permissions** — Explain owner/editor/viewer and who can do what.
4. **Inviting your team** — How to add and manage workspace members.
5. **A tour of the Explorer** — Orient a new user to the graph/exploration UI.
6. **Key terms and concepts** — A short glossary (object, relationship, proposal, revision).

## 2. Model fundamentals

1. **Object types and objects** — The core distinction and when to use each.
2. **Relationship types and relationships** — How connections are modelled.
3. **Attributes and attribute definitions** — Adding structured, free-form data to objects.
4. **Designing a good ontology** — Practical guidance on naming and structuring types.
5. **Proposals and revisions, explained** — Why canonical data is never written directly.
6. **Reading change history** — How to see what changed, when, and why.

## 3. Working with AI and change

1. **How AI assistance works in OnyxJar** — The Semantic Claim Invariant, in plain language.
2. **Create: building a model with AI help** — Walking through the Create operation.
3. **Reconcile: merging new evidence into your model** — Walking through the Reconcile operation.
4. **Understanding Evidence Graphs and claims** — What the AI can and can't assert on its own.
5. **Reviewing and adjudicating AI-proposed changes** — How to accept, edit, or reject proposals.
6. **When AI assistance isn't the right tool** — Guidance on manual vs. AI-assisted changes.

## 4. Data and publishing

1. **Importing a spreadsheet or CSV** — The ingestion workflow end to end.
2. **Resolving import identity issues** — Why an import blocks instead of partially importing.
3. **Import size and format limits** — Practical constraints to plan around.
4. **Publishing a Portable Explorer file** — What publishing produces and how to share it.
5. **Choosing what to include in a publication** — Scoping a publish to the right audience.
6. **Working with the offline Explorer file** — What recipients can and can't do with it.

## 5. Practical guides

1. **Renaming an object type without breaking links** — A common, slightly risky operation.
2. **Finding everything connected to a given object** — Using Explorer traversal effectively.
3. **Cleaning up duplicate objects** — A step-by-step dedup workflow.
4. **Setting up a recurring review cadence** — Keeping a model trustworthy over time.
5. **Preparing a model for a stakeholder demo** — Practical polish before sharing.
6. **Exporting data out of OnyxJar** — What's possible today and the recommended approach.

## 6. Troubleshooting and account help

1. **I can't sign in** — Common causes and fixes.
2. **My invite or verification email never arrived** — Troubleshooting steps.
3. **A proposal won't go live** — Common validation failures and how to read them.
4. **An import keeps failing validation** — Diagnosing the most common ingestion errors.
5. **Who to contact for help** — Pointing to the contact form and what to include.
